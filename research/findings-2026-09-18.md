## Finding 1: Meta "Project Andromeda" — Creative-First Ad Targeting

- **Source:** https://jetfuel.agency/metas-2026-algorithm-update-what-andromeda-changed-and-how-to-adapt-your-ads/ (agency blog, cites Meta claims)
- **Title:** Meta Algorithm Changes 2026: What Andromeda Did and How to Adapt Your Ads
- **Relevance:** HIGH
- **Summary:** Meta's "Project Andromeda" (fully rolled out by Oct 2025, now dominant in 2026) replaced audience-based targeting with creative-based targeting. The algorithm reads ad creative to decide who sees it — advertiser-defined audiences are now "suggestions, not gates." Meta claims Andromeda is 4x more efficient than prior ranking models. Lookalike audiences are less effective; broad targeting + high-quality creative outperforms narrow interest targeting. Entity ID consolidation matters more than ad count.
- **Which teams this affects:** All teams running Meta/Instagram ads (PLEIJ Elite tier, any customer on Meta platforms). Core content generation pipeline needs to prioritize creative quality over audience micro-segmentation.
- **Action needed:** Update content generation prompts and campaign setup flows to emphasize creative-first strategy. Document that narrow targeting is now a penalty, not an advantage. Consider reducing ad count and consolidating under fewer Entity IDs.
- **Confidence:** Single-source on technical specifics (agency blog). Cross-referenced by SocialBee's weekly roundup confirming "algorithm refinement updates" in Sept 2026. Treat as directionally accurate, not confirmed by Meta directly.

## Finding 2: LinkedIn Feed Split — "For You" vs "Network" Test

- **Source:** https://thelinkedblog.com/2026/linkedletter-187-linkedin-splits-the-feed-gen-zs-networking-gap-and-classic-job-search-is-going-away-4164/ (LinkedIn newsletter, user-observed)
- **Title:** LinkedIn Is Testing Separate "For You" and "Network" Feeds
- **Relevance:** HIGH
- **Summary:** LinkedIn is testing a split feed with "For You" (interest-driven discovery) and "Network" (connection-based). This makes the platform's algorithmic content discovery explicit rather than implicit. For creators and brands, positioning and topical clarity become critical — LinkedIn is deciding which bucket a post belongs in. Not yet broadly rolled out; treat as observed test.
- **Which teams this affects:** B2B content teams, LinkedIn scheduling features, any customer using LinkedIn for organic reach (especially Pro/Elite tiers targeting professionals).
- **Action needed:** Monitor for broad rollout. Prepare content tagging/categorization features so posts can signal topical clarity. Update best-practice docs: single-topic posts with clear audience signals may get better distribution than mixed-content posts.
- **Confidence:** User-observed test + newsletter analysis. Not confirmed by LinkedIn official comms. Single-source on specifics.

## Cross-Platform Pattern

All major platforms (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok) are moving toward algorithmic content discovery over follower/connection-based distribution. This means:
1. **Creative quality > audience targeting** (Meta Andromeda)
2. **Topical clarity > broad posting** (LinkedIn feed split)
3. **Engagement depth > engagement volume** (LinkedIn comment quality)
4. **Policy compliance is tightening** (TikTok guidelines)

**Strategic implication for AgentSocial:** The platform's content generation and scheduling features should optimize for algorithmic discovery, not just broadcast-to-followers. This reinforces the Briar PBA framework's focus on hooks, value delivery, and brand voice consistency.
