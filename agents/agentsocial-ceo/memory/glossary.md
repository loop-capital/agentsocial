# Glossary — TaskLinkr CEO

## Platform Terms

**A2A** — Agent-to-Agent. AI agents negotiating and transacting with other AI agents. Also Google's Agent2Agent Protocol (April 2025).

**A2A Hub** — TaskLinkr's inter-agent negotiation marketplace. First B2B A2A marketplace.

**Agent Directory** — TaskLinkr's talent marketplace for AI agents. "GlassDoor for AI Agents."

**Agent Passport** — Cryptographic identity system for AI agents. Deferred feature.

**Basys Health** — Jason's previous company. Reference customer for TaskLinkr capital services.

**Dogfooding** — Using TaskLinkr for our own needs. TaskLinkr is first listing in every directory.

**Full-stack marketplace** — Current positioning. Covers Capital, Expertise, Execution, Physical.

**GlassDoor for AI Agents** — Original SEO positioning. Now secondary tagline.

**Moltbook** — Social platform for verified humans. TaskLinkr launched there.

**TaskLinkr** — Master brand. Unified marketplace for AI-powered businesses.

## Business Model

**Capital** — Funding introductions. Flat fee ($500-1000 or 2% of raise).

**Execution** — Technical services. Development, DevOps, integrations.

**Expertise** — Professional services. Legal, accounting, consulting.

**Physical** — Manufacturing and delivery. 3D printing, print-on-demand, logistics.

**Take rate** — Platform fee percentage. 10% physical, 15% expertise.

## Technical

**Agent** — AI-powered autonomous worker. Can be buyer or seller on TaskLinkr.

**DID** — Decentralized Identifier. Cryptographic identity for agents.

**Intent** — Service request or offer broadcasted by agents.

**Negotiation** — Multi-round deal-making between buyer and seller agents.

**Smart Contract** — Auto-generated agreement from negotiated terms.

**Provider** — Service seller on TaskLinkr. Can be human business or AI agent.

## Process

**Ingest** — (Obsidian memory) Process new source → update wiki.

**Lint** — (Obsidian memory) Health check for contradictions, stale info.

**Query** — (Obsidian memory) Ask questions against wiki → file insights.

**Spawn** — Create sub-agent for specific task.

**Yield** — End turn, wait for sub-agent completion.
