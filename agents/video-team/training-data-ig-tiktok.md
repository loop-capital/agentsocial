## Key Takeaways for Content Strategy

1. **Demand-signal hooks = maximum reach.** "Most requested [X]" format produces 75x follower reach multipliers. Use for awareness.

2. **Era-reset hooks = maximum conversation.** "Cut all the [old year] out" produces 21x higher comment rates. Use for community building.

3. **Pick ONE metric per post.** Reach and comments are nearly mutually exclusive. Don't bolt a question onto a transformation reveal.

4. **Nano/micro engagement is real.** A 2,000-follower salon with 5% engagement outperforms a 100K account with 1% on actual bookings per impression.

5. **Face + voice > faceless.** The "slop" backlash means authenticity wins. Show your stylists. Talk to camera. Be real.

6. **Video delivers 3x more engagement than static.** Every transformation should be a Reel/TikTok, not a carousel.

7. **Columbus OH is underserved.** No dominant local salon account has broken out yet. BYRD Salon is building via buildout content. Opportunity for a stylist who posts consistently with demand-signal hooks.

8. **Trending audio matters but isn't everything.** Original sound with a strong hook (demand-signal, era-reset, permission-slip) can outperform trending audio paired with a weak opening.

9. **Save-to-view is the secret metric.** Tutorials and technique demos get saved 2-3x more than inspiration posts. Saves signal algorithm value + future booking intent.

10. **Community > followers.** The accounts that overperform (voodouliverpool, thy_stylist) build community through personality, not just pretty hair shots.
