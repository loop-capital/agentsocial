## Hair Color Trend Forecast 2026

### Spring 2026
- **Cashmere sand** — warm, soft, dimensional blonde
- **Polished copper** — refined, not brassy
- **Soft liquid black** — deep, glossy, almost blue-black
- **Multidimensional brunette** — layers of light within dark
- **Expensive blonde** — lived-in, low-maintenance golden

### Summer 2026
- **Rum punch copper** — tropical, warm, vibrant
- **Lived-in espresso** — deep base with soft face-framing
- **Sun-bleached blonde** — natural, effortless
- **Bronde** — brunette + blonde, best of both
- **Honey highlights** — warm, golden, sun-kissed

### Fall 2026 (Current)
- **Rich auburn** — classic fall, dimensional red-brown
- **Copper** — THE color of 2026 fall, everywhere
- **Chocolate brown** — deep, warm, glossy
- **Maple highlights** — amber, warm gold on dark base
- **Burnt sienna** — warm, earthy, sophisticated

### Winter 2026 (Projected)
- **Liquid noir** — deepest brunette, almost black
- **Icy platinum** — cool blonde, frosty
- **Burgundy** — deep wine red
- **Chestnut** — warm brown with red undertones
- **Frosty highlights** — cool-toned, winter-inspired
