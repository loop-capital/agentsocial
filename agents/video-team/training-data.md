## Hook Patterns That Work (Salon Industry)

### Top-Performing Hook Formulas (Ranked by Engagement)

**Tier 1 — Highest Engagement (+50-86% over baseline)**

1. **The Transformation Reveal**
   - "Wait for the reveal 😍"
   - "The after is INSANE"
   - "You won't believe this transformation"
   - Pattern: Tease the result → Show the process → Reveal

2. **The Unexpected Result**
   - "This isn't what you think it is"
   - "I didn't expect this to turn out so good"
   - "Watch what happens when..."
   - Pattern: Set expectation → Subvert it → Deliver better

3. **The Before/After Split**
   - "POV: You're getting the best haircut of your life"
   - "Left or right? 👀"
   - "Same person. Different energy."
   - Pattern: Side-by-side comparison → Dramatic reveal

**Tier 2 — High Engagement (+25-50%)**

4. **The How-To Hook**
   - "3 things your stylist isn't telling you"
   - "Stop doing this to your hair"
   - "The #1 mistake people make with [X]"
   - Pattern: Problem → Solution → CTA

5. **The Trending Audio Hook**
   - [Trending sound] + "Me running to my stylist after seeing this"
   - [Trending sound] + "When the color hits different"
   - Pattern: Trending audio → Relatable salon moment

6. **The Quick Tip**
   - "Did you know your shampoo is doing THIS?"
   - "One thing that changed my hair forever"
   - Pattern: Surprising fact → Quick explanation → Save prompt

**Tier 3 — Moderate Engagement (+10-25%)**

7. **The Staff Spotlight**
   - "Meet [Name], our [role] 👋"
   - "A day in the life of a [role]"
   - Pattern: Introduction → Behind-the-scenes → CTA

8. **The Product Review**
   - "Testing the viral [product] so you don't have to"
   - "Is [product] worth the hype? Let's find out"
   - Pattern: Setup → Test → Verdict

### Hook Timing (Critical)
- **First 1 second**: Visual hook (before/after split, dramatic color, surprising action)
- **First 3 seconds**: Text hook appears on screen
- **First 5 seconds**: Audio hook delivered or trending sound kicks in
- Videos with hooks in first 3 seconds are **72% more likely to go viral**

## Video Structure Templates (Top-Performing Formats)

### Format 1: Before/After Transformation (Highest Engagement)
```
0-1s: HOOK — Split screen or dramatic before shot + text overlay
1-3s: TEASE — "Wait for the reveal" or dramatic setup
3-20s: PROCESS — sped-up process footage (color application, cutting, styling)
20-25s: REVEAL — Dramatic slow-motion reveal of final result
25-28s: REACTION — Client reaction (if available) or stylist satisfaction
28-30s: CTA — "Book your transformation → link in bio"
```
**Performance**: +86% engagement over baseline. Best format for salons.

### Format 2: Educational Quick Tip (Highest Saves)
```
0-1s: HOOK — "Did you know..." or "Stop doing this..."
1-5s: PROBLEM — Show the wrong way or common mistake
5-15s: SOLUTION — Show the right way with step-by-step
15-25s: RESULT — Show the difference
25-30s: CTA — "Save this for later" + "Follow for more tips"
```
**Performance**: +38% shares, +45% saves. Good for building authority.

### Format 3: Behind-the-Scenes (Highest Comments)
```
0-1s: HOOK — "A day in the life..." or "Come with me..."
1-10s: INTRO — Stylist introduction, morning setup
10-25s: PROCESS — Authentic behind-the-scenes, client interactions
25-30s: CLOSING — "Love what we do? Book at [link]"
```
**Performance**: +30% comments. Builds personality and trust.

### Format 4: Staff Spotlight (Highest Follows)
```
0-1s: HOOK — "Meet [Name], our [role]"
1-10s: INTRO — Name, role, specialty, fun fact
10-20s: DEMO — Brief clip of their best work
20-28s: PERSONALITY — Why they love what they do
28-30s: CTA — "Book with [Name] → link in bio"
```
**Performance**: Good for multi-stylist salons. Builds personal connection.

### Format 5: Trending Audio (Highest Reach)
```
0-1s: HOOK — Trending audio starts, visual matches the beat
1-10s: SYNC — Cuts timed to audio beats (color changes, reveals)
10-15s: PEAK — Most dramatic moment synced to audio peak
15-30s: OUTRO — Smooth transition to CTA
```
**Performance**: Highest reach potential, requires staying current with trends.

## Color Grading Reference (Platform-Specific)

### Instagram Reels
- Warm, slightly saturated (+10-15% warmth)
- Lifted shadows for brightness
- Consistent color temperature across all content
- Avoid overly cool tones (performs -15% on Instagram)

### TikTok
- Higher contrast, more saturated
- Edgier, less polished look
- Faster cuts (2-3 second average shot length)
- Authentic over produced (over-produced -20% engagement)

### YouTube Shorts
- Neutral-to-warm grade
- Higher production value expected
- Educational content benefits from clear, bright lighting
- Slightly longer shots (3-4 second average)

### GBP Video
- Professional, clean, neutral
- Consistent lighting across all GBP videos
- No dramatic color shifts
- True-to-life color representation

## Music/Audio Patterns

### Top-Performing Audio Categories (Salon Content)
| Category | Engagement Impact | Best For |
|----------|------------------|----------|
| Trending audio (current) | +35% reach | Transformations, BTS |
| Lo-fi chill beats | +15% saves | Process videos, tutorials |
| Upbeat pop/indie | +20% shares | Before/after, reveals |
| Acoustic warm | +10% comments | Staff spotlights, reviews |
| ASMR (scissors, water, product sounds) | +25% watch time | Process videos |

### Audio Rules
- Music at -12dB to -18dB below speech
- Speech always dominates
- Fade in: 1-2s at start
- Fade out: 1-2s at end
- Never use copyrighted music
- Trending audio: check weekly, replace when trend fades

## Negative Patterns (What NOT To Do)

### Low-Performing Patterns
| Pattern | Why It Fails | What To Do Instead |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| No hook in first 1s | 65% scroll past | Always open with visual hook |
| Overly polished/stock look | Feels inauthentic (-20%) | Show real salon, real people |
| Generic "book now" CTA | No urgency | "Only 3 spots left this week" |
| Text overlays in generation | Artifacts, unreadable | Add text in post-production |
| Single long video | Drop-off after 15s | Break into series of shorts |
| Talking head without visual change | Boring after 5s | B-roll cuts every 3-5s |
| Aggressive sales pitch | Turns off (-30% engagement) | Lead with value, soft CTA |
| Wrong aspect ratio | Looks unprofessional | Always match platform spec |
| Low-resolution output | -40% engagement | Minimum 720p, target 1080p |
| No CTA at all | Missed conversion | Always include soft CTA |
