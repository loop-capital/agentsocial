**Body:**
```

We've received it and our system is now analyzing whether it violates the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA).

Here's what happens next:

   in TCPA cases. They work on contingency—you pay nothing.

3. CASE PROCEEDING
   Your matched attorney handles everything: filing, negotiation,

   When the case settles or wins, you receive $500-$1,500 per 
   violation. Attorneys deduct their fees from the award, not from you.

Keep forwarding any spam texts you receive. Each one is a potential 
separate violation.

Questions? Reply to this email.

report@spamsuit.co
```
