### Post 4: Educational
**Post:**
Quick TCPA primer for non-lawyers:

The Telephone Consumer Protection Act (1991) restricts telemarketing calls and texts. Violations include:
• Texts without prior express consent
• Texts sent using auto-dialers without consent
• Missing opt-out mechanisms

Statutory damages: $500 per violation, $1,500 if willful.

If you're receiving spam: forward to report@spamsuit.co. We'll validate and connect you with counsel at no cost.
