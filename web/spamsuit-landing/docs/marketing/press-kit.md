# SpamSuit Press Kit

## One-Paragraph Company Description

**For media use:**

SpamSuit is a consumer protection platform that empowers individuals to monetize illegal spam text messages under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). The service allows anyone to forward unsolicited marketing texts to report@spamsuit.co, where automated validation identifies TCPA violations and connects consumers with qualified attorneys who pursue claims on contingency. With statutory damages set at $500-$1,500 per violation, SpamSuit transforms a daily annoyance into enforceable legal claims—at zero cost to consumers. Founded on the principle that economic incentives should align with consumer protection, SpamSuit bridges the gap between overwhelmed enforcement agencies and individual rights holders, creating a scalable solution to the 55+ billion spam texts plaguing U.S. consumers annually.

### TCPA Legal Framework

- **$500** statutory minimum per violation (47 U.S.C. § 227)
- **$1,500** per willful violation
- **4 years** statute of limitations from violation date
- **1991** law enacted (Telephone Consumer Protection Act)
- **2012** FCC clarification on text message coverage
- **No cap** on number of violations per defendant

### SpamSuit Impact

- **$500-$1,500** consumer payout per validated violation

- **$0** consumer cost (attorneys work on contingency)
