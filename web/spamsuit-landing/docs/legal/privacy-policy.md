## 1. Who We Are

SpamSuit helps people who have received spam text messages and calls report violations and connect with attorneys who may be able to help them seek compensation under laws like the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA).

## 2. What Data We Collect

When you use SpamSuit, we collect:

- **Spam messages and call records** — The content of spam texts, caller IDs, and timestamps
- **Sender information** — Phone numbers that sent you spam
- **Device information** — Basic technical data to prevent fraud and improve our service
- **Optional account information** — Email address and phone number if you choose to create an account

## 3. What We DON'T Collect

- We do **not** collect your name, address, or other personal information unless you explicitly opt-in
- We do **not** access your contacts, photos, or other personal files
- We do **not** collect information about non-spam messages

## 4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

- **Validate spam violations** — Confirm reported messages violate telemarketing laws
- **Detect patterns** — Identify repeat offenders and spam campaigns
- **Match you with attorneys** — Connect you with lawyers who can help pursue your case
- **Build evidence packages** — Compile documentation attorneys need for litigation
- **Improve our service** — Make SpamSuit better at catching spammers

## 5. Data Sales: What We Sell and What We Don't

**We NEVER sell your personal information.**

We may sell **aggregated, anonymized data** such as:
- Statistics about spam trends (e.g., "10,000 reports of Bank X spam this month")
- Pattern analysis that cannot identify individual users
- Industry reports on spam volume and types

This data has all personally identifiable information removed.

## 6. Who We Share Data With

**Attorneys:** We share your specific spam reports with attorneys ONLY when:
- You explicitly consent to be matched with an attorney
- The attorney agrees to our confidentiality terms
- The sharing is necessary to pursue your potential case

**Data Buyers:** Only receive fully anonymized, aggregated data as described above.

**Service Providers:** We use trusted third-party services for hosting and analytics. They are contractually bound to protect your data.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** — Request a copy of your data
- **Delete** — Request deletion of your personal data (see Data Retention below)
- **Opt-out** — Stop receiving communications from us
- **Withdraw consent** — Change your mind about attorney matching at any time

**GDPR/CCPA:** If you are in the EU or California, you have additional rights under these laws. Contact us to exercise them.

## 8. Data Retention

- **Spam reports** — Kept for up to 7 years if connected to active or potential litigation
- **Personal information** — Deleted upon request, except where we must keep it for legal compliance
- **Aggregated/anonymized data** — Kept indefinitely as it cannot identify you

## 9. Children's Privacy

SpamSuit is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## 10. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure. Please use strong passwords and keep your account credentials safe.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or through the app.
