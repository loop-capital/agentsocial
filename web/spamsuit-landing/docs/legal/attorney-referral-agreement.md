_This agreement is between SpamSuit ("Referrer") and [ATTORNEY/FIRM NAME] ("Attorney")_

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

## 1. Purpose of This Agreement

This agreement establishes a referral relationship where SpamSuit refers potential Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and related telemarketing violation cases to Attorney for evaluation and potential representation.

## 2. Referral Relationship

### 2.1 What SpamSuit Provides
SpamSuit will:
- Screen and validate spam violation reports from consumers
- Identify reports that may constitute actionable legal violations
- Provide Attorney with evidence packages for validated violations
- Refer matching victims to Attorney when both parties consent

### 2.2 What Attorney Provides
Attorney will:
- Evaluate referred cases within [NUMBER] business days
- Accept or decline cases based on Attorney's professional judgment
- Provide representation to accepted clients under separate attorney-client agreements
- Report settlement outcomes as required by this agreement

## 3. Fee Structure

### 3.1 No Upfront Fees
SpamSuit receives no upfront payment from Attorney. All compensation is contingency-based on successful case outcomes.

### 3.2 Contingency Fee Split
When Attorney successfully recovers compensation for a referred client:

- **Attorney keeps:** Approximately 67% of the gross attorney fees
- **SpamSuit receives:** Approximately 25% of the gross attorney fees as a referral fee
- **Client receives:** The settlement amount less attorney fees (as per Attorney's contingency agreement)

*Note: Exact percentages may vary by jurisdiction requirements and specific case circumstances.*

### 3.3 Payment Timing
Attorney will pay SpamSuit's referral fee within [NUMBER] days of receiving settlement funds.

## 4. Case Acceptance Process

### 4.1 Case Review
Upon receiving a referral, Attorney will:
- Review the evidence package within [NUMBER] business days
- Determine if the case meets Attorney's criteria for acceptance
- Notify SpamSuit of acceptance or declination

### 4.2 Client Contact
If Attorney accepts the case:
- Attorney will contact the client directly within [NUMBER] business days
- Attorney will provide their own attorney-client agreement
- SpamSuit is not party to the attorney-client relationship

### 4.3 Declined Cases
If Attorney declines, SpamSuit may refer the case to other attorneys in our network.

## 5. Evidence Package

For each referred case, SpamSuit will provide:
- Screenshots or copies of spam messages
- Call logs and timestamps
- Sender/caller identification information
- Pattern analysis showing potential TCPA violations
- Client contact information (with consent)
- Any other relevant documentation

Attorney acknowledges that SpamSuit does not provide legal opinions on case viability.

## 6. Settlement Reporting

Attorney agrees to:
- Report all settlements of referred cases to SpamSuit within [NUMBER] days
- Provide settlement amounts (gross and net)
- Calculate and remit referral fees based on reported settlements
- Maintain records of settlements for audit purposes

## 7. Exclusivity

### 7.1 First Right of Refusal
Attorney receives first right to evaluate and accept cases that match their criteria.

### 7.2 Non-Exclusive Relationship
This agreement is not exclusive. SpamSuit may work with other attorneys. Attorney may accept referrals from other sources.

### 7.3 Geographic/Practice Area Limits
Attorney's first right applies only to:
- [SPECIFY GEOGRAPHIC AREA if applicable]
- Cases meeting Attorney's specified criteria

## 8. Term and Termination

### 8.1 Term
This agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for [NUMBER] months/years, automatically renewing unless terminated.

### 8.2 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate with [NUMBER] days written notice.

### 8.3 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate immediately for:
- Material breach of this agreement
- Violation of professional ethics rules
- Failure to maintain required licenses

### 8.4 Post-Termination
Attorney remains obligated to:
- Pay referral fees for cases accepted before termination
- Report settlements for accepted cases

## 9. Confidentiality

### 9.1 Client Information
Attorney agrees to:
- Maintain confidentiality of all client information received
- Use client information only for case evaluation and representation
- Comply with all applicable privacy laws and attorney ethics rules

### 9.2 SpamSuit Information
Both parties agree to keep confidential:
- Business methods and processes
- Fee structures and financial arrangements
- Non-public data and analytics

## 10. Indemnification

### 10.1 By Attorney
Attorney agrees to indemnify and hold SpamSuit harmless from any claims arising from:
- Attorney's representation of referred clients
- Attorney's professional malpractice or misconduct
- Attorney's breach of this agreement

### 10.2 By SpamSuit
SpamSuit agrees to indemnify and hold Attorney harmless from any claims arising from:
- SpamSuit's misrepresentation of case facts (to the extent SpamSuit knew or should have known)
- SpamSuit's breach of this agreement
