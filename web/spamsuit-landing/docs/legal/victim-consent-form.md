## Purpose of This Form

This form gives SpamSuit permission to share information about spam messages you've received with attorneys who may be able to help you seek compensation. Please read carefully before signing.

## What You're Consenting To

By signing below, you consent to the following:

### 1. Sharing Your Violation Data With Attorneys

You give SpamSuit permission to share with attorneys:
- Screenshots and content of spam messages you've reported
- Phone numbers that sent you spam
- Dates and times of spam messages/calls
- Your contact information (email and phone)
- Any other evidence you've provided about the spam

**Why:** Attorneys need this information to evaluate whether you have a case and to represent you if you choose to proceed.

### 2. Anonymized Data Aggregation

You give SpamSuit permission to include your spam reports in:
- Aggregated statistics about spam trends
- Pattern analysis that identifies repeat offenders
- Reports we may share or sell to third parties

**Important:** Your personal information (name, email, phone) is removed before inclusion in any aggregated data. Only the spam content and patterns are included.

**Why:** This helps identify spammers and supports our business so we can continue offering free services to victims like you.

### 3. Attorney Contact

You understand that:
- SpamSuit may match you with attorneys who handle spam litigation
- The attorney will contact you directly to discuss your case
- You are not obligated to hire any attorney we match you with
- You can decline representation at any time

### 4. Contingency Fee Arrangements

You understand that:
- Attorneys who may contact you typically work on a "contingency fee" basis
- This means you pay nothing upfront
- The attorney only gets paid if they win your case
- If you win, the attorney takes a percentage of your recovery (typically 25-40%)
- SpamSuit may receive a referral fee from the attorney (separate from your recovery)

**Note:** The exact terms will be in a separate agreement between you and the attorney.

### 5. Authorization to Pursue Legal Action

If you choose to hire an attorney:
- You authorize them to pursue legal action on your behalf
- You agree to cooperate with reasonable requests for information
- You understand you may need to provide additional documentation
- You may be asked to testify or participate in legal proceedings

### 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Decline** this consent form and still use basic SpamSuit reporting features
- **Withdraw consent** at any time by contacting us
- **Request deletion** of your personal data (though we may retain some information for legal compliance)
- **Ask questions** before signing — contact privacy@spamsuit.com

### 7. No Guarantees

You understand that:
- SpamSuit does not guarantee an attorney will take your case
- SpamSuit does not guarantee you will receive any compensation
- SpamSuit is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice
- Any legal relationship is between you and the attorney you choose
