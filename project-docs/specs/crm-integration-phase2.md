# CRM Integration — Phase 2 Plan

## Status: SAVED — Future Consideration

## Overview

Phase 2 expands CRM integration to include GHL SaaS mode support and agency commission program.

## Agency Channel Strategy

**Value Proposition for Agencies:**
- Agencies using GHL for their clients can sell AgentSocial as an add-on service
- Increases service offerings and value for their clients
- Makes their jobs easier (automated social media management)
- Earn commission on AgentSocial subscriptions

**Implementation:**
1. **GHL SaaS Mode Support**
   - White-label AgentSocial within GHL platform
   - Custom branding (logo, colors, domain)
   - Requires Agency Pro plan ($497/mo)

2. **Agency Commission Program**
   - Agencies earn % of AgentSocial subscription revenue
   - Tiered commission structure based on client volume
   - Self-service onboarding for agency clients

3. **Multi-Location Support**
   - One agency account → many salon locations
   - Location-based reporting and analytics
   - Parent-child account structure

## Timeline

**Phase 2 Start:** After Phase 1 completion + 30 days validation
**Dependencies:** 
- Phase 1 CRM integration stable
- Agency onboarding process defined
- Commission tracking system built

## Questions to Resolve

3. Agency support model (self-service vs. managed)
