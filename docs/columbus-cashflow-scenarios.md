## Key Assumptions

### Stylist Profile (Columbus Average)
- Monthly product spend: $1,800
- Mix: 60% color/chemical, 40% retail/styling
- Current waste rate: 12% (expired, trend changes)
- Current order size: $1,800 (monthly)

### TaskLinkr Model
- Weekly ordering frequency
- 25% manufacturer discount (negotiated group rate)
- 2% waste rate (fresher inventory)
- $8/delivery fee (Amazon Flex bulk rate)
- Hub sorting: 2 min per order

### Economic Environment
- Cost of capital: 10% (opportunity cost)
- Implicit storage cost: $50/month (space under booth)

## Break-Even Analysis

**For Individual Stylist:**
- TaskLinkr beats SalonCentric at any order frequency
- Even with $8/week delivery fee, savings from 25% discount + waste reduction dominate

**For Group Minimum:**
- 10 stylists = viable for most indie brands ($1,800-2,000/week)
- 25 stylists = viable for all brands including Oribe ($4,500/week)
