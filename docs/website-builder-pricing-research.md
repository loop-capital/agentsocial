## Key Price Points
- **Budget tier**: $0–5/mo (limited, with ads or branding)
- **Starter tier**: $9–17/mo (custom domain, basic features)
- **Professional tier**: $20–30/mo (advanced features, e-commerce)
- **Enterprise tier**: $50–300/mo (scale, multi-site, advanced commerce)

## Recommended AgentSocial Pricing Strategy
Bundle website builder into existing plans as a value-add rather than separate pricing:

| AgentSocial Tier | Social Features | Website Builder | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Basic scheduling | 1-page landing only | $0 |
