# Manus Integration — User Interview Guide

## Target User Profiles

### 1. AI Agent Operators (n=1-2)
- People who run AI agents (Claude, GPT, custom) to manage social accounts
- Pain: Agents generate generic content; no feedback loop from ad performance data
- Key question: Would Meta-derived insights make your agent's output measurably better?

### 2. Solopreneur Creators (n=1-2)
- Individuals running their own brand on social (coaches, creators, consultants)
- Pain: No time or budget for deep ad research; writing copy is the bottleneck
- Key question: Would trend/audience data save you research time and improve your posts?

### 3. Social-First Small Businesses (n=1-2)
- Local/regional businesses spending $500-5K/mo on Meta ads
- Pain: Waste budget on underperforming creative; can't afford agency-level insights
- Key question: Would competitor and audience insights increase your ad ROI?

---

## Interview Script (30 min)

### Intro (2 min)
"We're building a feature that connects your social media content tool to Meta's advertising data — so your AI-generated posts and ads are informed by what's actually performing on Meta right now. I want your honest take on whether this solves a real problem for you."

### Current Workflow (5 min)
1. Walk me through how you create social content today — from idea to publish.
2. How do you decide what to write about? What informs your messaging?
3. Do you look at competitor ads or audience data before creating content? If so, how?
4. What's the most painful part of this process?

### Feature Reactions (15 min)
*Describe each feature and show mockup/wireframe if available.*

5. **Insight-Powered Copy:** "Imagine your AI tool could look at the top-performing ads in your industry on Meta and use those patterns to write better copy for you. How valuable is that? What concerns do you have?"
6. **Trend Scanning:** "What if the tool told you 'these 5 creative trends are emerging in your vertical this week'? Would you act on that? How would you want it presented?"
7. **Audience Insights:** "If the tool suggested targeting parameters based on your actual Meta audience data, would you trust it? What would make you trust it more?"
8. **Competitor Analysis:** "How interested are you in seeing what your competitors are running on Meta? Would you change your strategy based on that info?"
9. **Toggle:** "We're thinking of a simple on/off toggle for Meta insights. Does that feel right, or would you want more granular control (e.g., turn on for copy but off for targeting)?"

### Pricing & Quota (5 min)
10. Free tier gives you ~300 credits/day (roughly 2 trend scans + 3 copy optimizations). Does that feel sufficient for your use case?
11. Would you pay for more? What's a fair monthly price for unlimited access?
12. How would you feel if the tool fell back to 'regular AI' when credits run out?

### Wrap-Up (3 min)
13. On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to use this feature if it shipped tomorrow?
14. What's the one thing that would make this a must-have vs. nice-to-have?
15. Anyone else I should talk to?

## Success Metrics (Post-Interview)

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Feature interest | ≥7/10 average rating | Interview Q13 |
| Time saved on research | ≥30% reduction | Self-reported vs. baseline |
| Ad CTR improvement | ≥15% increase | A/B test: insights-on vs. insights-off copy |
| Copy acceptance rate | ≥80% of insight-enhanced copy kept | Platform analytics |
| Toggle adoption | ≥60% of users enable within 7 days | Feature flag analytics |
| Paid conversion | ≥5% of free users upgrade for more credits | Billing data |
| NPS for feature | ≥40 | In-app survey after 2 weeks |
