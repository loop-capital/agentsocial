## Description SEO Keywords (use naturally, don't stuff)

**High-intent local search terms to include naturally:**
- "near me" contexts (e.g., "best balayage in [city]")
- Service names: balayage, highlights, keratin, extensions, blowout
- Location qualifiers: Columbus, [your city], downtown
- Modifiers: affordable, luxury, walk-in, appointment-only

**Do NOT keyword-stuff the business name.** Google will suspend you.

## Photo Requirements

- Minimum 10-25 photos for optimal ranking
- Mix of: exterior, interior, team photos, before/after, service shots
- Add new photos weekly to signal activity
- Use Google Posts weekly with CTAs

## Review Strategy

- Minimum 4.5 stars for AI Overview inclusion
- Target 5-10 new reviews/month
- Respond to ALL reviews within 24 hours
- AI Overviews cite businesses with 4.5+ stars and recent review activity

## NAP Consistency (Critical)

- Business name, address, phone must be IDENTICAL across:
  - Google Business Profile
  - Website
  - Yelp, Facebook, Instagram
  - All directory listings (50+)
- Even small differences (Rd vs Road) hurt ranking
