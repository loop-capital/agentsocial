## Services

### Hair

- **Haircut & Style** — Precision cutting and styling for all hair types. Includes consultation, shampoo, cut, and blow-dry.
- **Balayage & Hair Color** — Custom balayage, highlights, ombre, single-process color, color corrections, and fashion colors.
- **Blowout Services** — Professional blowouts for smooth, voluminous, or textured finishes.
- **Natural Beaded Rows™ Hair Extensions** — The most natural, lightweight, and comfortable extension method. No glue or tape. Fullness without damage. PLEIJ certified NBR specialists.
- **Keratin Smoothing Treatments** — Frizz elimination and brilliant shine. Results last 3–6 months.
- **Head Spa & Scalp Treatments** — Trichology-based treatments that detoxify follicles for thick, sustainable hair growth.
- **Hair Loss Solutions** — Calecim Advanced Hair Restoration System, scalp treatments, and personalized regrowth plans.
- **K-Tip Hair Extensions** — Individual keratin-bonded extensions for seamless, natural-looking length and volume.

### Skin & Makeup

- **Custom Facials** — Deep cleansing, anti-aging, hydrating, and corrective facials tailored to your skin type.
- **HydroFacial** — Multi-step hydradermabrasion: cleanse, exfoliate, extract, and hydrate in one treatment.
- **Chemical Peels** — Professional peels for fine lines, acne, hyperpigmentation, and texture.
- **Microblading & Permanent Makeup** — Semi-permanent cosmetic tattooing for brows, eyeliner, and lip color. Lasts 1–3 years.
- **LED Light Therapy** — Non-invasive treatment for acne, inflammation, and anti-aging using red, blue, and near-infrared wavelengths.

### Med Spa & Wellness

- **Botox & Wrinkle Relaxers** — FDA-approved neuromodulator injections by licensed medical professionals.
- **Dermal Fillers & Injectables** — Hyaluronic acid fillers for lip augmentation, cheek enhancement, and facial contouring.
- **Skinvive** — The first and only FDA-approved HA microdroplet injection for cheek smoothness and hydration.
- **IV Therapy** — Customized intravenous vitamin and nutrient therapy for hydration, immunity, energy, and recovery.
- **Microneedling** — Collagen-inducing treatments for skin texture, scarring, and anti-aging.

### Nails

- **Manicures** — Classic, gel, and luxury spa manicures with cuticle care and hand massage.
- **Pedicures** — Classic, spa, and luxury pedicures with callus treatment and foot massage.
- **Nail Enhancements** — Gel, acrylic, and dip powder for long-lasting nails.

### Massage & Acupuncture

- **Massage** — Swedish, deep tissue, hot stone, and prenatal massage by licensed therapists.
- **Acupuncture** — Traditional and cosmetic acupuncture for pain relief, stress reduction, and facial rejuvenation.

### Bridal

- **Bridal Hair Services** — On-location and in-salon bridal styling.
- **Bridal Trial Services** — Pre-wedding trial runs to perfect your look.

### Children's Services

- Haircuts and styling services for kids in a welcoming environment.
