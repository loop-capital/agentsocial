# PLEIJ Salon — Brand Voice & Content Profile

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: PLEIJ Salon
- **Location**: Columbus, Ohio
- **Type**: Full-service salon (hair, balayage, keratin, extensions, color)
- **Phone**: 614-665-1751
- **Square**: Production booking integration
- **Voice Agent**: Gisele (AI receptionist)

## Target Audience (ICP/IGP)
- **ICP**: Professional women 28-45 in Columbus metro, $75K+ HHI, values quality over discount, busy schedule
- **IGP (group chat)**: Bridesmaids groups, moms transitioning careers, professionals comparing salons
- **Pain points**: Bad color experiences, "salon chair anxiety," inconsistent results, feeling judged at upscale salons
- **Dream outcomes**: Confidence, compliments, "where do you get your hair done?", low-maintenance color that lasts

## Content Pillars
1. **Authority/Expertise** — Color education (balayage, keratin, tonal correction)
2. **Transformation** — Before/after, client stories, process videos
3. **Salon Culture** — Team, vibe, behind-the-scenes, Columbus community
4. **Trust/Proof** — Reviews, FAQs, myth-busting, "why PLEIJ"

## Voice Signature
- **Tone**: Warm, confident, never condescending. Like your smartest friend who happens to do hair.
- **Do**: Specific claims ("3 hours, not 5"), proper nouns ("Columbus," "Short North"), tangible outcomes ("brass-free blonde"), fifth-grade reading level
- **Don't**: Em-dashes, "success," "great results," "transform your look," generic salon clichés, before-and-after without the story
