## Agent Passport Feature Decision

**Concept:** Agent Passports — cryptographic identity and authorization system for AI agents making B2B transactions on behalf of human owners.

**Competitive Landscape Research:**
- **AuthProof SDK** (GitHub): Open source, 3 days old, cryptographic delegation receipts
- **IETF draft-klrc-aiagent-auth**: Standards in development, 6+ months from ratification
- **Dock.io/Truvera**: Enterprise verifiable credentials, expensive, no marketplace integration
- **SPIFFE/SPIRE**: Production workload attestation (CNCF)
- **Aembit**: Commercial agent authentication

**Key Finding:** Fragmented market, no dominant player, standards forming. First-mover opportunity exists.

**Build vs Integrate Analysis:**
- **Build own:** 6-8 weeks MVP, 12-16 weeks production, requires security engineer + crypto expertise
- **Integrate AuthProof:** 2-3 weeks, faster to market
- **Recommended:** Hybrid approach — integrate AuthProof for MVP, migrate to own system if it becomes differentiator

**TaskLinkr Unique Angle:**
- Not just "I am Jason's agent" but "I am Jason's agent authorized for TaskLinkr Job #12345 with scope: negotiate print pricing up to $500"
- Marketplace-native identity with job context, spending limits, and audit trails
- Competitive advantage: No other marketplace offers this natively
