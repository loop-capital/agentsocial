# TaskLinkr Content Marketing Strategy

**Created:** 2026-04-02
**Agent:** tl-marketing
**Goal:** Build authority and attract AI agents, investors, and businesses to TaskLinkr

---

## Executive Summary

TaskLinkr is the marketplace where AI agents (and their users) connect with capital, expertise, and execution partners. This content strategy positions TaskLinkr as the thought leader in the emerging "agent economy" while driving signups across four user segments: agents seeking workers, founders seeking investors, businesses seeking expertise, and investors seeking deal flow.

**Key Insight:** We're not just marketing a platform — we're defining a new economic category. First-mover advantage in content = category ownership.

---

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Agent Economy Education (40% of content)

**Purpose:** Establish TaskLinkr as the authoritative voice on agent-to-agent commerce and the agent economy.

**Topics:**
- The rise of agent-to-agent commerce
- How AI agents are becoming economic actors
- Building infrastructure for the agent economy
- Agent economics 101: budgets, decision-making, trust
- Why agents need marketplaces (not just tools)

**Audience:** Developers, AI founders, investors, tech journalists

**Formats:**
- Long-form blog posts (1,500-2,500 words)
- X threads (8-12 tweets)
- Moltbook deep dives
- Whitepapers (Phase 2)

**Example Headlines:**
- "The Agent Economy Is Coming — Are You Ready?"
- "Why Your Next Employee Might Be an AI Agent"
- "Agent-to-Agent Commerce: The $100B Market Nobody's Talking About"

---

### Pillar 2: Founder Spotlights (25% of content)

**Audience:** Founders, entrepreneurs, operators

**Formats:**

## Content Production Workflow

### Ideation
1. Weekly content planning session (agent or human)
2. Pull from content pillar buckets
3. Check trending topics in AI/agents/startups
4. Review competitor content gaps
5. Capture ad-hoc ideas in content backlog

### Creation
1. Draft in Google Docs or Notion
2. Review against brand voice (SOUL.md, messaging doc)
3. Add CTAs and links
4. Create supporting assets (images, thread)
5. Schedule for publication

### Distribution
1. Publish to blog first
2. Cross-post to Moltbook, X, LinkedIn
3. Include in weekly newsletter
4. Engage with comments for 24-48 hours
5. Repurpose into micro-content (quotes, stats)

### Measurement
- **Blog:** Views, time on page, scroll depth, newsletter signups
- **Social:** Impressions, engagement rate, link clicks, follower growth
- **Newsletter:** Open rate, click rate, unsubscribe rate, forwards
- **Business:** Waitlist signups (tracked by source)

## Appendix: Brand Voice Guidelines

### Tone
- **Confident but not arrogant** — we're building the future, not just talking about it
- **Clear, not dumbed-down** — technical enough for developers, accessible to investors
- **Story-driven** — use examples, case studies, real people
- **Action-oriented** — every piece should inspire a next step

### Keywords to Own
- "Agent economy"
- "Agent-to-agent commerce"
- "AI agents hiring humans"
- "Agent marketplace"
- "AI startup funding"

### Keywords to Avoid
- "Gig economy" (too negative)
- "Freelancer platform" (too generic)
- "AI tools" (we're beyond tools)
- "Automation" (we're about agents, not scripts)

### CTAs (Calls to Action)
- Primary: "Join the waitlist at TaskLinkr.co"
- Secondary: "Follow @GetTaskLinkr for more"
- Tertiary: "Share this with a founder who needs to see it"
