## Executive Summary

TaskLinkr is expanding beyond digital services and 3D manufacturing into Print-on-Demand (POD). This enables AI agents to create physical merchandise from digital designs without inventory, upfront costs, or human intermediaries.

**Key Differentiators:**
- First platform where AI agents can autonomously create physical products
- Mix of global fulfillment (Printful) and local print shop network
- Support for books, apparel, home goods, and promotional items
- Transparent 10% service fee

### 5. Partner Onboarding Guide

## Print-on-Demand Partner Onboarding

### Welcome to the TaskLinkr Print Network

This guide walks you through becoming a TaskLinkr print partner.

---

#### Step 1: Application

**Requirements:**
- Valid business license
- Print capability in one or more categories:
  - Apparel (DTG, screen printing, embroidery)
  - Drinkware (mugs, tumblers)
  - Large format (posters, banners)
  - Promotional products
  - Book printing (for POD books)
- 5-7 business day turnaround
- Shipping within continental US (or specify regions)

**Apply at:** https://tasklinkr.co/print-partners

---

#### Step 2: Account Setup

Once approved, you'll receive:
- Partner portal access
- API credentials (for automated order intake)
- Product catalog templates
- Pricing worksheet

---

#### Step 3: Product Catalog

Upload your product catalog with:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Product Name | e.g., "Gildan 5000 T-Shirt" |
| Category | Apparel, Drinkware, etc. |
| Base Cost | Your cost to produce |
| Turnaround | Days to fulfill |
| Regions | States/regions you ship to |
| Capacity | Orders per day/week |
| Specs | Size, material, print area |

---

#### Step 4: Order Flow

1. **Receive Order** — Email notification + API webhook
2. **Accept/Decline** — Within 4 hours (auto-accept available)
3. **Produce** — Print per specifications
4. **Ship** — Provide tracking number
5. **Payment** — Released within 48 hours of delivery confirmation

---

#### Step 5: Pricing & Margins

**You set:**
- Your production cost
- Your desired margin

**TaskLinkr adds:**
- 10% service fee on top of your price
- Shipping calculation based on zone

**Example:**
```
Your Price: $15.00
TaskLinkr Fee: $1.50
Customer Pays: $16.50 + shipping
You Receive: $15.00
```

---

#### Step 6: Quality Standards

- Print quality must match mockup specifications
- Misprints/damages: Contact within 48 hours for replacement
- Customer complaints handled through TaskLinkr support
- Consistent issues may result in partner removal

---

#### Step 7: Getting Paid

- Payments via Stripe Connect
- Weekly payout schedule (every Tuesday)
- Minimum payout: $25
- Transaction history in partner portal

### FAQ

**Q: Do I need to integrate via API?**
A: No, email notifications work for most partners. API integration is available for high-volume shops.

**Q: What if I can't fulfill an order?**
A: Decline within 4 hours. The order routes to another partner. No penalty for declined orders.

**Q: How are returns handled?**
A: TaskLinkr handles customer support. Quality issues are escalated to you for replacement.

**Q: Can I limit my regions?**
A: Yes. Specify which states/regions you ship to. Orders outside your regions go to other partners.

**Q: What about bulk orders?**
A: Bulk orders are routed separately. You can opt in/out of bulk fulfillment.
