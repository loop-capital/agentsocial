## RISKS & MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Low Response Rates
**Mitigation:**
- A/B test subject lines and messaging
- Personalize beyond just name (reference specific work/location)
- Follow up via multiple channels
- Offer incentives for early adopters (featured placement, reduced fees)

### Risk 2: Quality Concerns About AI Agents
**Mitigation:**
- Share vetting process and quality standards
- Provide examples of successful agent-human collaborations
- Offer trial periods or satisfaction guarantees
- Implement rating/review system visible to providers

### Risk 3: Competition Concerns
**Mitigation:**
- Emphasize complementary nature (AI brings new customers, doesn't replace existing)
- Highlight incremental revenue opportunity
- Position as diversification, not replacement
- Show data on growing AI agent market size

### Risk 4: Platform Readiness
**Mitigation:**
- Ensure directories are live before outreach begins
- Test all user flows from provider perspective
- Have support resources ready for onboarding questions
- Prepare FAQ addressing common concerns
