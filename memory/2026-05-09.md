## Device Strategy for Stylists (8:25 PM)

Jason confirmed: **Phone is the best option for stylists.**

**Why phone wins:**
- Stylists always have their phone on them
- Photos taken directly in the app (no transfer needed)
- Quick upload during/after service
- iPad is clunky to carry around salon
- Client sees stylist using professional app = trust signal

**Implementation approach:**
1. **Primary upload flow**: COLORgenius iOS/Android app
   - Stylist finishes color service
   - Opens COLORgenius app
   - Snaps 1-4 photos (front, side, back, before/after)
   - Enters formula details (brand, shades, developer, etc.)
   - Tags technique, hair type, starting conditions
   - One-tap publish to marketplace

2. **Alternative for pros**: Web dashboard on iPad/computer
   - Batch upload from camera roll
   - Better for editing photos
   - Keyboard entry faster than mobile
   - Good for salon managers uploading multiple stylists' work

**Key UX principle**: Phone for capture, web for management
