# 2026-05-11 — Business Model Pivot: SaaS + Done-For-You Service

## Decision: Offer Zoca-style DFY Service on Top of AgentSocial

**Why:** Zoca proves salons will pay premium ($300-800+/mo) for "we do it for you" vs. self-serve tools. We already have the content engine + builder. We just need to wrap it with hands-on service.

**Model:** Hybrid — keep self-serve AgentSocial as-is, add higher-tier "AgentSocial Pro" that includes full DFY marketing management.

**Key Intel:** Jason's wife used Zoca at $299/mo and just cancelled because she believed we could replicate it. This confirms the market and gives us real pricing data.

---

## Service Tiers (Proposed)

### Tier 1: AgentSocial Core (Self-Serve)
**Price:** $49-79/mo
**What's Included:**
- Social media scheduler + AI content generation
- Multi-platform posting (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X)
- Basic analytics dashboard
- Content calendar
- AI caption writer + hashtag suggestions
- Access to SiteFlow builder (DIY)

### Tier 2: AgentSocial Pro (Semi-Done-For-You)
**Price:** $199-299/mo
**What's Included:**
- Everything in Core, PLUS:
- **Google Business Profile management** (weekly posts, Q&A, photo updates)
- **Review management** (auto-respond to reviews, solicit new ones)
- **Local SEO basics** (citations, listings optimization)
- **AI Booking Assistant** (chat widget on website, SMS replies)
- **Monthly content strategy call** with dedicated account manager
- **AI-generated social content** (we create + they approve, we post)
- **Automated rebooking SMS/email campaigns**
- **Simple landing page** built in SiteFlow (Zoca offers "starter website" — we do better)

### Tier 3: AgentSocial Elite (Full DFY — Zoca Competitor)
**Price:** $499-799/mo
**What's Included:**
- Everything in Pro, PLUS:
- **Full social media management** — we create, schedule, post everything
- **Google Business Profile daily optimization**
- **24/7 AI chat/SMS/voice booking assistant**
- **Advanced local SEO** (neighborhood targeting, competitor analysis)
- **Paid ads management** (Google Local, Meta ads) — *this is likely what Zoca runs for that "starter website"*
- **Monthly performance report + strategy adjustment**
- **Priority human support** (dedicated Slack/Discord channel)
- **Multi-location management**
- **Website updates & optimization**

## Competitive Advantage Over Zoca

1. **We have the content engine** — they probably use generic AI; ours is trained on salon/beauty content
2. **SiteFlow builder** — they only offer basic 1-page landing page (pleijsaloncolumbus.zoca.com); we have full drag-and-drop builder with multi-page support
3. **Own domain** — Zoca hosts on their subdomain (client.zoca.com); we let clients use their own domain
4. **Existing Square integration** — already handling billing
5. **Clipify integration** — video repurposing they don't have
6. **Transparent pricing** — they force "book a call"; we show prices upfront
7. **Real pricing intel** — wife paid $299/mo and cancelled, confirming the price point is acceptable but the value wasn't clear enough

## Insights from Wife's Cancellation
- **Price point**: $299/mo is acceptable to salon owners
- **Pain point**: She felt she could replicate it herself → implies lack of perceived differentiation/lock-in
- **Our fix**: Make the value undeniable — show concrete ROI (booking increases, review growth, time saved)
- **Upsell path**: Start her on Core ($49), upgrade to Pro when she sees results
