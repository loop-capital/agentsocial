### Product Suite

#### 1. SpamSuit (Lead Recovery)
**Purpose:** Help victims get paid for TCPA violations
**Revenue:** 20-30% of attorney settlements
**User Promise:** "Report spam, get $500-$1,500 per violation"

#### 2. SpamShield (Blocking Service)
**Purpose:** Proactive spam prevention
**Revenue:** Consumer subscriptions ($5-10/mo), carrier API fees

**Purpose:** Free app to collect spam data from users
**Revenue:** Sell aggregated data to carriers, security firms, researchers

```
SpamCapture App ──→ Users report spam ──→ Database grows
```
