### 2. Remove Bad Reviews (Priority)
Google won't remove reviews just because we ask. Here's the process:

#### Reviews to Flag for Removal
Flag each one through Google Business Profile → Reviews → 3-dot menu → "Report review"

**Flagging reasons:**
- Reviews from competitors with no client record → **Conflict of interest**
- Reviews with no specific details about service → **Spam**
- Reviews with profanity or harassment → **Inappropriate content**

#### What to include in your flag report:
"This reviewer has never been a client of PLEIJ Salon. We have checked our booking system and product purchase records and found no record of this person ever visiting our salon or purchasing any products. This appears to be a fraudulent review left to harm our business."

#### Escalation if Google doesn't remove:
1. Flag again after 7 days if no action
2. Call Google Small Business Support: **844-491-9663**
3. If still denied, consider legal demand letter
